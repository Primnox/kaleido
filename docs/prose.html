Prose

A new kind of interface. No buttons, sliders, icons or menus. The whole interface is one sentence about what's happening, and every control is a word in it. The film stays untouched underneath, and VLC still plays everything.

Click a word to change itDrag a word to slide itScroll on a word to step through its choicesPlain words are just words, lit words are controls
01WatchingMove your hand and the sentence fades up over the bottom of the film. It describes the moment, and each lit word is a control.
The Long Way North, 41 minutes in. Pause. It's loud, in English, at normal speed.
02Dragging a phraseThe time phrase is the seek bar. Pull it sideways and it rewrites itself while the film's own frames gather under it.
The Long Way North, 41 minutes in. Pause. It's loud, in English.
03Choosing a wordWords with choices show them waiting above and below. Scroll to roll through them, the way a dial would.
Coral Season, near the end. It's quiet, in FrenchJapaneseEnglishno words, at normal speed.
04Turning it upVolume is an adjective. Drag it and it walks through its own words, all the way past the limit VLC is famous for.
Night Drive, an hour in. Pause. It's loud, in English, at normal speed.
05PausedThe lights come up and the sentence moves to the middle of the room. It says where you are in words you'd use yourself.
Paused at the river crossing.
Carry on, go back a little, or find a moment.
06Reaching for moreEverything VLC hides in its menus is a sentence you can edit. Hover a lit word and its effect shows on the film.
The words are a little late.
The sound is louder than loud, with the bass lifted.
The colours are warmer and the picture is a bit brighter.
It speaks Japanese, and the words are large.
07Coming backOpening the app is a sentence too. Your last film waits under the lamp, and the sentence offers what makes sense right now.
You stopped The Long Way North a little past the middle. Carry on, start over, or watch something else.

Tide Pools, the next one.
Night Drive, half watched.
Or anything in Videos, or a file.
08The endThe film is over, so the sentence tells the story of your watching and offers the next one.
